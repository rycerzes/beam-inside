By depth band. Coherent: Beam judged its own label coherent. Significant: steering moved outputs toward a theme beyond effect-matched random controls. Judge: a blind judge picked the expert's steered output from three, given its label (chance 33%).

    How this was made

    Beam ran in vLLM (NVFP4 weights, 4× RTX PRO 6000 on HF Jobs) with a plugin that adds per-request pushes to the residual stream, router changes and exact expert bans. Each expert's top two write directions, read from its weights, were pushed at doses calibrated per layer on effect (ΔNLL), on three chat prompts and an effort prompt. A direction is significant when its outputs drift toward one theme more than 95% of random-direction controls that move Beam by a similar amount. Beam then named each direction from its top tokens (via HF Inference Providers), and a DeepSeek judge took a blind three-way test. Bans set the router's correction bias to −1000, which removes an expert exactly.

    Limits. One greedy sample per prompt; early layers (0–23) are underdosed and their few hits are at chance with the judge; layers 48+ collapse at the needed dose and often have no fair control; 478 experts lost their sweep data to a time limit; the competition-math arms are 100 problems. Details in the write-up.
